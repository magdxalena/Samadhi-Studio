Welcome ✨

Samadhi Studio is a space for slowing down and returning to yourself through therapeutic touch, conscious breath and mindful movement.

I'm Magdalena, a certified massage therapist and yoga teacher. My path into this work began in 2019, when I was working as a graphic designer and turned to yoga, meditation, and massages to calm my mind and ease physical tension. What started as a personal practice became something much deeper ~ I went on to complete trainings in bodywork, yoga, breathwork, and somatic practices, and now share these tools with others.

I believe in a holistic approach, where mind and body are interconnected. Every session is guided by your individual needs, creating a space where you can simply arrive as you are.
