Samadhi Studio

Welcome ̴ this a space for slowing down and returning to yourself through therapeutic touch, conscious breath and mindful movement.

I'm Magdalena, a certified massage therapist and yoga teacher. My path into this work began in 2019, when I was working as a graphic designer and turned to yoga, meditation, and massages to calm my mind and ease physical pain. What started as a personal practice became something much deeper ~ I went on to complete several trainings and now share my knowledge with others.

I believe in a holistic approach. I like to get to know each person I work with on a deeper level and design an experience for their individual needs.

Offerings

You can choose between two types of offerings.

Individual Sessions focus on one practice and are ideal if you're looking for a massage, private yoga class or breathwork session.

Immersive Sessions begin at two hours and offer a more spacious, personalized experience. Together, we create a session that may combine massage, yoga, breathwork, meditation, Reiki, or somatic practices, depending on your intentions and needs.
